Everyday

A quiet place to write your hopes, remember your moments, and make each day your own.

By Abhi

Coming soon to the App Store

No perfect entries. No pressure. Just a little something to look back on.

One small page a day

Hopes

Start the day with a line or two about what you hope it brings.

Memories

Come back later and write down what you’d like to remember.

A daily mark

Draw something that represents today with your finger or Apple Pencil, or choose a little illustration from a library of hundreds of marks.

Your year in dots

Home shows the whole year as a grid of dots. Each day you mark turns into a tiny keepsake you can tap to read again.

Gentle extras, all optional

Streaks & badges

Watch a little flame grow as you journal on consecutive days, and collect milestones from your first entry to 100 days.

Reminders

Choose a morning nudge for your Hopes and an evening one for your Memories, at the times you like.

Make it yours

Add your name and pick a profile mark from twenty little animals and creatures.

Export to PDF

Turn your saved entries into a single PDF whenever you like, and keep it or share it wherever you choose.

Kept private on your own phone

There’s nothing to sign up for. Everyday has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and your journal is stored on your iPhone rather than on a server we run. Read the full details in the Privacy Policy.

Good to know

Everyday is made for iPhone and requires iOS 17 or later. Need a hand? Visit Support.
