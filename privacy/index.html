Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Applies to Everyday 1.0 for iPhone

The short version. Everyday is a private journal. Your entries, drawings and settings are stored on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no advertising, no tracking, and no cloud sync run by the developer. We don’t collect your journal or any other personal information.

What Everyday stores on your iPhone

Everything below lives in Everyday’s private storage on your device:

  • Journal entries. For each day you write: the date, your Hopes, your Memories, and your daily mark (either a drawing you made or a preset mark you chose).
  • Profile. The optional name you enter and the profile mark you pick.
  • Preferences and app state. Reminder on/off choices and times, your display choices (decorative marks, streak, badges), and small flags such as whether you’ve finished the introduction. A random number and your start date are also stored on your device; they are used only to arrange the optional decorative marks, and they are never sent anywhere or used to identify you.

Your streak and badges are calculated on your device from your saved entries. They aren’t stored anywhere else.

This information is used only to run the app on your iPhone. Everyday doesn’t send it to the developer or to anyone else.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking, or cross-app or cross-site profiling. Everyday contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.
  • No developer-operated servers or cloud sync. The app doesn’t send your journal over the internet.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information. We don’t have any to sell or share.

Everyday’s privacy manifest declares that the app does not track you and does not collect data.

Reminders (optional)

Everyday can remind you to write, once in the morning and once in the evening. These are local notifications: iOS schedules and shows them on your iPhone at the times you choose. They don’t come from a server, and Everyday asks for notification permission only when you choose to enable reminders. If you’ve added a name, your reminder text may include it (for example, “Good morning, Sam!”), and like any notification it can appear on your lock screen depending on your iOS notification settings. You can turn reminders off in Everyday’s Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

PDF exports (only when you ask)

In Settings, you can export your saved entries as a single PDF. The PDF is created on your iPhone only when you tap export, and then iOS shows its share sheet so you decide where it goes, such as Files, Mail, Messages, AirDrop, or a printer. Everyday removes its temporary copy when you close the share sheet.

Once you save or send a PDF, that copy is outside Everyday. It is stored, protected and handled by the app or service you chose, under that app’s or service’s own policies. Your journal contents can leave your iPhone this way, but only by your action.

iPhone backups

Everyday doesn’t run its own backup or sync. However, like most apps, its data can be included in the backups of your iPhone that you make with iCloud Backup or to a computer, depending on your iOS settings. Those backups are created and held by Apple (under Apple’s terms) or on your computer, according to your iOS settings, and not by us. We can’t access them. If you restore an iPhone from a backup, your journal comes back with it.

Apple’s optional diagnostic reporting

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide the developer with crash reports and other diagnostic information about how Everyday is performing. That sharing is controlled by you, collected and managed by Apple, and subject to Apple’s privacy policy. Everyday doesn’t add your journal text or drawings to these reports, and the developer doesn’t use them to identify you.

Deleting your data

  • Delete the app. Deleting Everyday from your iPhone removes its stored journal, profile and preferences from that device, and iOS cancels its scheduled reminders.
  • Backups and exports. Deleting the app does not delete copies that exist elsewhere, such as iPhone backups (you can manage or delete these in iOS Settings, or on your computer) and any PDFs you saved or shared. Please remove those yourself if you want them gone.
  • Within the app. You can edit your writing, clear or replace a daily mark for today, and change your name or profile mark. Everyday 1.0 doesn’t include a “delete everything” button.

Because we never receive your journal, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Everyday collects no personal information from anyone, including children. There are no accounts, and the app doesn’t send your journal anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If Everyday’s handling of information changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that sends data off your device, we’ll update this page and its effective date, and describe the change in the app’s update notes before it takes effect.
