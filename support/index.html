How to create an entry

  1. On Home, tap the add (+) button at the bottom, or tap today’s dot, to open today’s page.
  2. Tap the Hopes card, write what you hope today brings, then tap Save writing.
  3. Tap the Memories card and do the same for what you’d like to remember. You can write it later in the day.
  4. Under Leave a mark for today, draw a mark or choose one (see Drawing & preset marks).
  5. Tap the check mark to head back to Home.

Each part saves when you save it, so you can come back to today’s page and edit Hopes, Memories or the mark whenever you like. If you leave a writing screen with the back button instead of saving, those changes are discarded.

How to view previous entries

  • Tap a dot. Days with a saved entry open when you tap them.
  • Press and hold the dot grid, then slide your finger to move between days. A date bubble appears; tap it to open that day.
  • Hop between days. Inside an entry, a row of nearby days sits along the bottom. Tap one to go there.
  • Read long entries. Tap a Hopes or Memories card, or its expand button, to read the full text.
  • Change the year. Press and hold the year at the top of Home, then slide left or right to browse other years.

Past entries are for reading. The one exception: if one of the last three days has writing but no mark, an Add a mark button lets you choose a preset mark for it. Days with nothing saved show “No entry for this day,” and future dates can’t be opened.

Drawing & preset marks

Draw a mark

  1. In the mark card, tap the draw button.
  2. Draw inside the canvas with your finger or Apple Pencil.
  3. Use undo and redo to step back and forward, or clear to start over. A small Home preview shows how your mark will look at its size on the calendar.
  4. Tap save when you’re happy with it.

Choose a preset mark

Tap the add button (the plus) to open the mark library. Browse by category, such as Food, Nature, Home, Activities, Travel, Work, Feelings and Objects, or use Search marks. Tap a mark to use it for the day.

Change or remove a mark

On today’s page, tap the clear button under the mark and confirm to remove it, then draw or choose a new one. Everyday keeps one mark per day, so a new mark replaces the old one.

Streaks & badges

Streaks

Your streak counts consecutive days on which you completed all three parts: Hopes, Memories and a daily mark. If today isn’t finished yet, your streak keeps counting through yesterday. Miss a whole day and it starts fresh. Tap the flame on Home to see your streak.

Turn the streak on or off in Settings → Progress → My streak. It’s on by default, and new journals show it once a first streak exists.

Badges

Badges celebrate how many different days you’ve journaled, not how many in a row: your first entry, then 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100 days. A day counts once it has saved writing or a mark. They’re hidden by default. Turn on Settings → Progress → My badges, then tap View my milestones to see what you’ve earned and how many days are left to the next one. Hiding badges never removes what you’ve earned.

Notification settings

Go to Settings → Notifications. There are two reminders, Morning Hopes (7:00 AM unless you change it) and Evening Memories (9:00 PM unless you change it).

  • Use each toggle to turn a reminder on or off.
  • Tap the time beside a reminder to choose a different time.
  • The first time you enable reminders, by turning one on or tapping Enable notifications, iOS asks for permission. If you said no, Settings shows Open Settings, which takes you to Everyday’s notification settings in iOS.

Reminders are local to your iPhone. They repeat every day at your chosen time, in your current time zone.

PDF export

  1. Open Settings → Export Journal.
  2. Tap the export button. Everyday prepares one PDF of all your saved entries, with dates, Hopes, Memories and daily marks.
  3. In the iOS share sheet, choose where to send it, such as Save to Files, Mail, AirDrop or Print.

The export is made only when you ask, and only you choose where it goes. If you have no saved entries yet, Everyday tells you there’s nothing to export.

Data backup & deletion

  • Where your journal lives. On your iPhone only. There’s no Everyday account and no developer-run cloud copy.
  • Backups. Everyday doesn’t make its own backup. Your iPhone’s regular iCloud or computer backups may include its data, and restoring from one brings your journal back. To keep an extra copy, export a PDF.
  • Changing phones. Restore your new iPhone from a backup of your old one, or use the iOS transfer tools. Everyday has no sign-in to carry your journal across.
  • Deleting. Deleting the app removes its data from that iPhone. It does not remove existing iPhone backups or PDFs you saved or shared. Everyday 1.0 has no in-app “delete everything” button, so if you want a fresh start, delete the app.

Heads up: deleting the app deletes your journal from the device. If you want to keep your entries, export a PDF first.

More details are in the Privacy Policy.

Troubleshooting & FAQs

I’m not getting reminders
  • In Everyday, open Settings → Notifications and make sure the reminder is on.
  • If Settings says notifications are disabled, tap Open Settings and allow notifications for Everyday.
  • Check that no Focus or Do Not Disturb setting is hiding them, and that your iPhone’s time and time zone are correct.
  • Try changing the reminder time by a minute, or toggle it off and on to reschedule it.
My writing or drawing didn’t save

Everyday shows an “Unable to Save” message if something goes wrong. Try again, and check that your iPhone has free storage space. Make sure you tap Save writing (or save in the drawing screen). Leaving with the back button discards unsaved changes. If it keeps happening, restart the app and contact us.

I can’t open a day, or it says “No entry for this day”

Only today and days with saved writing or a mark can be opened. Future dates are view-only. If you wrote something that should be there, check that you’re on the right year at the top of Home.

Can I edit an old entry?

Past entries are read-only. You can edit today’s Hopes, Memories and mark. For the last three days, if an entry has writing but no mark, you can add a preset mark to it.

My streak went back to 0 (or isn’t showing)

A streak counts days with Hopes, Memories and a mark all saved, so a day missing any of the three breaks it. If the flame isn’t on Home, check Settings → Progress → My streak. New journals show the streak only after the first one begins. Decorative marks on the calendar never count toward a streak.

The calendar shows little pictures on days I didn’t write

Those are optional decorative marks, purely for looks. They aren’t entries and can’t be opened. Turn them off in Settings → Appearance → Decorative marks.

The PDF export failed

If you see “There are no saved journal entries to export yet,” write an entry first. If a mark or the layout couldn’t be read, Everyday says so and your journal isn’t changed; try again. Exporting a large journal can take a moment, so wait for the progress indicator to finish.

I deleted the app. Can I get my journal back?

Only if your iPhone has a backup made while Everyday was installed. Reinstall Everyday and restore your iPhone from that backup. The developer doesn’t have a copy of your journal and can’t recover it.

What does shaking my iPhone do?

On Home, shaking your iPhone a few times starts a playful Gravity Mode where your marks tumble around. Shake again to return to the calendar. It’s turned off when Reduce Motion is enabled in iOS accessibility settings.

Is there an iPad, Mac or Android version, or sync between devices?

Everyday 1.0 is made for iPhone. It doesn’t sync between devices, because your journal stays on the iPhone where you wrote it.

Does Everyday support Dark Mode?

Everyday uses a light appearance in 1.0, whatever your iPhone’s system setting.
